# Privacy policy for cookies.txt

**Developer:** o11dev

cookies.txt reads browser cookies when you open the popup or export them. It uses the browser's cookie and active tab APIs to show a preview and create a local download or clipboard copy. Cookie data is not sent to a server by this extension.

The extension does not run analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Your browser may retain downloaded files, clipboard contents, and extension settings according to its own behavior.

The selected export format is stored locally in extension storage so it can be reused.

You can remove the extension at any time through your browser's extension manager.
